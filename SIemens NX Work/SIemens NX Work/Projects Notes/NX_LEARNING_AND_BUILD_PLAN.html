What "finished" means in November

  1. MCD runs the whole cell by itself — the engraved route (75.65 s) and the bypass (29.74 s), every rod, belt, the platform and all ten sensors.
  2. It is checked against the MATLAB model — MCD's sensor trip times beside the model's, each difference explained.
  3. It runs over OPC UA, driven by the replay server; from the real PLC too, if OPC UA gets switched on there.
  4. A screen recording of both routes, and a report chapter.

1, 2 and 4 depend only on you and NX. The live-PLC half of 3 depends on the lab (job 5), so the plan doesn't wait for it.

This is your WILP project — check its calendar for review or submission dates before 6 November. From 4 October you can show MCD running both routes by itself; from 11 October, the OPC UA run and the validation table as well. Both are complete, showable states.
