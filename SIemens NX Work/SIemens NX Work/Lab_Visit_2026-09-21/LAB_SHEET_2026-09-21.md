### Job 1 — Why is the engraving route never taken? (TIA Portal, read-only, ~20 min)

**What we know.** px7 works (the maintainer, 2026-09-11). Belt 2 does not
reverse. Re-extracting 2026-09-10 today gives this:
- four cycles reached prox 8 (conveyor 2 → prox 8 in 2.26 s, n = 4), and
  **none went on to SV7 or SV11**;
- three stopped at PLC step 16 and one at step 3;
- in C0004-165150 the block sat on prox 8 for 49 s;
- **prox 7 was being recorded in those cycles and never changed.** Conveyor
  2 stopped as the block left prox 8, 0.24–0.54 s after prox 8 fired.
  Conveyor 3 never came on.

**On 2026-09-07 the route worked**, in 7 real cycles.
- In the one that recorded prox 7 (C0003-180221), conveyor 2 stopped 0.52 s
  after prox 7 fired, and SV7 followed.
- Two other cycles that day also stalled at step 16. The prox sensors were
  not recorded then.

**The timings disagree with each other.**
- On 2026-09-07 the block reached prox 7 2.62 s after conveyor 2 started
  (n = 1).
- On 2026-09-10 it reached prox 8, 22 cm further on, in 2.26 s (n = 4).
- So either belt 2 ran at different speeds on the two days, or prox 7 fires
  late when it fires at all.
- A late prox 7 would also stretch the engraved belt-2 run, the model's
  largest known miss (3.00 s against 1.44 s). *Inference, not checked.*

**So the question narrows to: why does prox 7 sometimes not see the block?**
*Inference, not checked:* step 16 is the PLC waiting for prox 7. Both days'
stalls stop there, and the one cycle that recorded prox 7 went past it.

**How, at the cell first.**
1. Watch prox 7's LED as the block passes in an automatic cycle. Note where
   the block rides across the belt, and **where along the belt it is when
   the LED lights**. A video with the belt and the LED in frame settles
   both.
2. If the LED stays off:
   - measure prox 7's trip distance with the spare block (runbook E4);
   - measure the gap from its face to the block's path;
   - photograph the sensor and its bracket.

**Then in TIA Portal,** open **FC6** (the main sequence). Look at the networks
after prox 8 / prox 7, and read what the PLC waits for before it commands:
- **cylinder A, SV7** (the engraving route);
- **the bypass, SV11**.

While you're in there, look at:
- **step 16**: is it the wait for prox 7? And what stops conveyor 2 at
  prox 8?
- **`engraving_power` (%Q3.5)**: what switches it on, and does the route
  wait for it?
- **what starts the `T#10S`** beside `CMD_START`;
- **product selection**: which bit or HMI setting chooses engrave or skip?

If they aren't known yet, these three reads take a minute each while FC6 is
open:
- **D1:** what the `T#12S` on `pgm_cylA` is timed from;
- **D2:** the **scan time** (Online & diagnostics → Cycle time). The model
  assumes 10 ms;
- **D3:** whether outputs are written at the **end of the scan** or
  immediately (CPU properties).

**Write down.** For each condition: the network number and the condition as
written. **Photograph the screen.** For D1–D3, the value.

**What it settles.** Plan §4 item 1. This decides whether today can have any
engraved cycles at all.

## 6. Running the model at the cell (optional, MATLAB prompt)

From the `Factory_Simulation` folder:

```matlab
factory_help
```

Run a cycle yourself, with the live view:

```matlab
run_factory_simulation('Operator', 'console', 'Live', true)
```

It asks in the Command Window: **Enter** means engrave the 50 mm job, and
**n** means skip (the SV11 bypass). The laser then runs for 4.65 s on the
figure. **Press any key on the figure for ENG DONE.** Shift, Ctrl or Alt alone
don't count. If you close the figure, the Command Window takes over.

Try a laser time you just measured, without editing any file:

```matlab
Q = FactoryParams(); Q.OP.t_laser_50 = 12.3;   % your reading, in seconds
run_factory_simulation('Params', Q, 'Operator', 'console', 'Live', true)
```

The bypass cycle, for comparison with job 3:

```matlab
Q = FactoryParams(); Q.engrave_selected = false;
run_factory_simulation('Params', Q)
```
