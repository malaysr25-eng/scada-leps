## 4. Why no cycle reached `cycle_history`

Every good cycle was faulted out by this:

    Conveyor 2: run output ON, no encoder motion -- the run coil has been
    energised for 2.3 s and Current_Velocity_Fb still reads 0

**This is a false positive.** Conveyor 2's run coil is a station-enable, not a
motor-running signal: in cycle `C0004-155943` it was ON from 16:00:00.649 to
16:01:06.728 — **66.079 s** — spanning the plate rise, the engraving and the
plate fall, during which the axis is correctly stationary. The detector fires
whenever the coil exceeds `diagnostics.motion_grace_s: 2.0` with zero feedback,
so it fires on essentially every cycle.

The encoders work. `C0004-155943` recorded `conv1.current_velocity_fb` sweeping
−52.37 → −27.31 → +22.38 → **+64.10** → +16.29, matching 2026-09-10's peak
exactly.

Consequence: the fault drives `AUTO_RUNNING -> FAULT -> RECOVERY_REQUIRED`, the
cycle never closes as COMPLETED, no `cycle_history` row is written, and
`extract_cell_timings.py` cannot see it.

**This is a software fix in `config/config.json` (`diagnostics.motion_grace_s`,
or the detector's gating) and needs the maintainer. It is the single change that
would unblock STEP 3.**

## 8. Do not use the digital twin comparison

`data/twin_reference.json` describes itself as:

> "SYNTHETIC SAMPLE. Every timing in this reference is fabricated to exercise
> the integration. It is NOT output from the MATLAB model and no engineering
> conclusion may be drawn from it."
> `"source": "SYNTHETIC SAMPLE -- NOT FROM MATLAB", "synthetic": true`

So **every `DIGITAL TWIN MISMATCH` warning in these captures is noise**, and the
"nominal cycle 29.78 s" is invented. Its own caveats also record that SCALE is
confirmed incorrect, that 69 of 97 model parameters are unmeasured, that 12.5 s
of the 31.08 s nominal cycle is operator placeholder, and that the model has
5 cylinders and 4 proximity sensors where the real cell has 13 valves and 10.

Generate the real reference with `tools/export_twin_reference.m` before any
comparison against the LAB_VISIT sheet.

## 9. Resolution limit on every number here

`plc.poll_interval` is **0.25 s**. A 0.1 s recording cannot beat it. Measured
prox pulse widths of 247–272 ms are one poll wide, so **every interval carries
±0.25 s**, and at present belt speeds some prox hits may be missed entirely.
