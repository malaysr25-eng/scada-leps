Before you leave home

Rules at the machine

  • Read-only on every computer. Never write a PLC tag. Never change the PLC program, the portal code or its config. TIA Portal is for reading only: open blocks and watch tables, but do not download, force or modify anything.
  • Never type a password into anything we keep, and never into Physics_Framework.xlsx (it is in git). Write down only whether a login was needed.
  • SV7/SV8 and SV9/SV10 are latching valves. Every extend needs its retract before moving on, or the next cycle starts with a rod out.
  • Do not touch any flow-control needle valve. They set the rod speeds the model is timed against.
  • Do not change the PLC program or engraving_power. Only the person responsible for the cell switches things on.
  • The laser: follow the lab's own rules. Keep the enclosure closed while it runs, and never look into the beam. Film only from outside.
  • Manual button tests go after the automatic cycles, never in between. On 2026-09-10 a cycle record ran on into manual tests. The new extractor cuts those off, but a clean record needs no cut.

1

Why is the engraving route never taken?

~20 minTIA Portal · read-onlyplan §4 item 1
What we know

px7 works (the maintainer, 2026-09-11). Belt 2 does not reverse. Re-extracting 2026-09-10 today gives this:

  • four cycles reached prox 8 (conveyor 2 → prox 8 in 2.26 s, n = 4), and none went on to SV7 or SV11;
  • three stopped at PLC step 16 and one at step 3;
  • in C0004-165150 the block sat on prox 8 for 49 s;
  • prox 7 was being recorded in those cycles and never changed. Conveyor 2 stopped as the block left prox 8, 0.24–0.54 s after prox 8 fired. Conveyor 3 never came on.

On 2026-09-07 the route worked, in 7 real cycles:

  • in the one that recorded prox 7 (C0003-180221), conveyor 2 stopped 0.52 s after prox 7 fired, and SV7 followed;
  • two other cycles that day also stalled at step 16. The prox sensors were not recorded then.

The timings disagree with each other. On 2026-09-07 the block reached prox 7 2.62 s after conveyor 2 started (n = 1). On 2026-09-10 it reached prox 8, 22 cm further on, in 2.26 s (n = 4). So either belt 2 ran at different speeds on the two days, or prox 7 fires late when it fires at all. A late prox 7 would also stretch the engraved belt-2 run, the model's largest known miss (3.00 s against 1.44 s). Inference, not checked.

So the question narrows to: why does prox 7 sometimes not see the block? Inference, not checked: step 16 is the PLC waiting for prox 7. Both days' stalls stop there, and the one cycle that recorded prox 7 went past it.

How, at the cell
  1. Watch prox 7's LED as the block passes in an automatic cycle. Note where the block rides across the belt, and where along the belt it is when the LED lights. A video with the belt and the LED in frame settles both.
  2. If the LED stays off: measure prox 7's trip distance with the spare block (runbook E4), measure the gap from its face to the block's path, and photograph the sensor and its bracket.
Then in TIA

Open FC6 (the main sequence). Look at the networks after prox 8 / prox 7, and read what the PLC waits for before it commands cylinder A, SV7 (the engraving route) and the bypass, SV11.

While you're in there, look at:

  • step 16: is it the wait for prox 7? And what stops conveyor 2 at prox 8?
  • engraving_power (%Q3.5): what switches it on, and does the route wait for it?
  • what starts the T#10S beside CMD_START;
  • product selection: which bit or HMI setting chooses engrave or skip?

If they aren't known yet, these three reads take a minute each while FC6 is open:

  • D1: what the T#12S on pgm_cylA is timed from;
  • D2: the scan time (Online & diagnostics → Cycle time). The model assumes 10 ms;
  • D3: whether outputs are written at the end of the scan or immediately (CPU properties).
Write down

For each condition: the network number and the condition as written. Photograph the screen. For D1–D3, the value.

2

Five or more engraved cycles, with a stopwatch

~40 minrecorder runningonly if the engraver works
How
  1. Run each cycle from a cold start at the magazine.
  2. Write each cycle's start and end clock time in LAB_HOUR_LOG.md, and which job it engraved.
  3. For each cycle, stopwatch the four parts in the table below with laps. Use one video with a stopwatch in frame as the cross-check.
  4. Laser time: time the laser on → off at least three times for each job. Write down the job itself: file or design name, engraved size in mm, and speed and power if the engraver's software shows them. If the software shows a job timer, photograph it each time.
Why the job

The laser time depends on the job, so a time is only useful with its job. The model waits 4.65 s [PLH] in manual mode. The only footage with the laser on suggests at least about 3.0 s. Your readings will replace it.

One cheap extra

The portal does not record engraving_power (%Q3.5): the 2026-09-10 database has no such tag, so the recorder cannot time the laser. The PLC's output LEDs might, if they can be seen without opening anything. If %Q3.5's LED is visible, film it next to the laser once. It may only mean "engraver powered", not "laser firing", so note what it did.

If it doesn't work

Say so in the log, and skip to job 3. A cycle with the platform raised and the laser idle is still worth recording, but mark it laser idle. The seven dwells the model uses now are all of that kind.

Before you leave the lab

Running the model at the cell

Optional. At the MATLAB prompt, from the Factory_Simulation folder.

factory_help

Run a cycle yourself, with the live view:

run_factory_simulation('Operator', 'console', 'Live', true)

It asks in the Command Window: Enter means engrave the 50 mm job, and n means skip (the SV11 bypass). The laser then runs for 4.65 s on the figure. Press any key on the figure for ENG DONE. Shift, Ctrl or Alt alone don't count. If you close the figure, the Command Window takes over.

Try a laser time you just measured, without editing any file:

Q = FactoryParams(); Q.OP.t_laser_50 = 12.3;   % your reading, in seconds
run_factory_simulation('Params', Q, 'Operator', 'console', 'Live', true)

The bypass cycle, for comparison with job 3:

Q = FactoryParams(); Q.engrave_selected = false;
run_factory_simulation('Params', Q)
